## 🕹️ About

False Hero is a fast-paced, retro-styled soulslike action RPG developed by Torchlight Games and published by Ytopia. In a corrupted Land of the Gods, a former scholar must uncover a legendary mask and confront the spreading power of Death. Copy enemy attacks, chain them into lethal combos, explore interconnected regions, and face demanding bosses with distinct patterns. Released on September 22, 2026, the game emphasizes combat mastery, atmosphere, exploration, and meaningful choices over grinding or complex gear management.

False Hero is for players who enjoy demanding single-player soulslikes, expressive combat systems, dark fantasy worlds, and skill-focused boss fights.

## ✨ Features

- ⚖️ **Meaningful Choices** — Choose whether to serve the Gods or become an apostle of Death.
- 🔥 **Combo Combat** — Chain copied abilities into fast, adaptable combinations.
- 🗺️ **Connected World** — Explore corrupted swamps, ancient cities, eerie ruins, and other interconnected regions.
- 🔀 **Multiple Endings** — Shape the fate of the world through your decisions.
- 🎭 **Dark Fantasy** — Uncover the mystery behind a world where life-giving gods are threatened by Death.
- 👹 **Challenging Bosses** — Battle memorable creatures with distinct movesets and carefully designed encounters.
- ⚔️ **Attack Stealing** — Learn unique enemy attacks and wield them as part of your own combat style.
- 🎼 **Atmospheric Presentation** — Experience a retro-inspired visual style supported by a dramatic soundtrack.
